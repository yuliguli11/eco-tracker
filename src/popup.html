      How we estimate impact

      Eco Tracker counts tokens from your conversations and applies published LLM inference energy benchmarks:

      • Energy: ~0.002 kWh / 1K tokens
      • Water: ~10 mL / 1K tokens (cooling)
      • CO₂: ~0.47 g / 1K tokens (US grid avg)
      • Token cost: ~4 characters = 1 token

      Sources

      Estimates are based on:

      • Li et al. (2023) — "Making AI Less Thirsty"
      • Patterson et al. (2022) — "Carbon Footprint of ML"
      • IEA Electricity CO₂ intensity (US average 2023)

      Estimates only. Actual values vary by data centre, model, and region.
